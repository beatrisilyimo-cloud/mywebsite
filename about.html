School Overview

Namwai Secondary School is a community-based secondary school located in Namwai Ward, Tanzania. The school was established to provide accessible and quality secondary education to students from different social and economic backgrounds.

Since its establishment, the school has focused on nurturing academic excellence, discipline, self-confidence, and moral values. The school believes that education is the foundation for national development and personal success.

Vision

To become a centre of academic excellence that produces disciplined, innovative, and responsible students capable of competing nationally and internationally.

Mission

To provide quality education through effective teaching, strong discipline, modern learning facilities, and active participation in academics, sports, and community activities.

School Location

Namwai Secondary School is located in Namwai Ward, within Tanzania. The location is accessible by road and provides a quiet and safe learning environment suitable for students.

The surrounding community works closely with the school to support discipline, development projects, and student welfare.

Facilities

  • Classrooms
  • Science laboratories
  • Computer room
  • Library
  • Sports grounds
  • Clean water and sanitation
