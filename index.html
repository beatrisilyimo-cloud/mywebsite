Welcome to namwai Secondary School

Namwai Secondary School is a well-established academic institution committed to providing quality education to students in Tanzania. The school focuses on academic excellence, discipline, moral values, and talent development.which enable student to acqure knowlege for their life.

About Our School

Namwai Secondary School was founded with the aim of preparing students academically, socially, and morally. The school offers a supportive learning environment, well-trained teachers, modern classrooms, laboratories, and a library.

Our mission is to empower students to become responsible citizens, critical thinkers, and future leaders. The school promotes discipline, respect, cooperation, and innovation.

School Location

Namwai Secondary School is located in Tanzania,at kilimanjaro region at siha distrit within a peaceful and secure environment suitable for learning. The school is easily accessible by road and is surrounded by supportive community services.
